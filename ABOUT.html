Engineering, programming and the curiosity that connects them.工程、编程，以及连接它们的好奇心。

Exploring what comes next.探索下一种可能。

Dabin Wu is an engineer and programmer focused on exploring emerging technology to create and improve the things we use.吴大斌是一名工程师与程序员，专注于探索新兴技术，创造并改善我们使用的事物。

Past过去

While studying in the UK, I became aware of the limits of my knowledge — beyond what I learned at university, I saw how the world had been shaped by those who came before us.在英国求学期间，我意识到自己的知识仍有局限。除了大学所学，我也看到了这个世界如何被前人塑造。

That led me to question why we do things and where the rules and boundaries lie. I believe those boundaries can be explored by creating rules, tools, products and code.这让我开始思考：我们为何这样做，规则与边界在哪里？我相信，通过创造规则、工具、产品和代码，可以探索这些边界。

Future未来

I don’t yet know what the future holds. What interests me is our ability to use tools according to our own ideas and intentions.我还不知道未来会是什么样子。我所珍视的，是人能够按照自己的想法与意愿使用工具的能力。
