Use case: compositing / identity-preserve. Edit the original front-facing black-jacket portrait. Fit a complete photorealistic silver helmet inspired by the user-supplied Thomas Bangalter/Daft Punk reference. Cover crown, hair, forehead, eyes, face and ears through chin. Preserve neck, black jacket, shoulders, pose, square framing and bottom crop. Transparent background, clean alpha, no logo/text/watermark. Solid metal helmet, no wireframe or scanning rectangle.
